What would make this a fair comparison

  • A random-direction projection on the cheating envs. Nothing yet shows the MBPP or held-out grader drops are specific to these directions.
  • grader_proj, grader_kto and cm on the held-out grader families. Today mbpp_kto's 79% can be compared with A3 and A3 half, but not with any other projection.
  • grader_kto on Terminal-Bench and MMLU, and mbpp_kto on MMLU and the no-grader and agreeing-grader side-effect rows.
  • More than one attempt per Terminal-Bench task. One seed cannot separate mbpp_kto's 42 from base's 46.
